# Content Plan — Dialed In Electric
**Goal:** Write like a real contractor. No AI fluff, no cheesy templates.  
**Voice:** Direct. Confident. Local. Technical without being jargon-heavy.

---

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### What it sounds like
- "We show up, wire it clean, and leave the place better than we found it."
- "Generac certified. Pad, transfer gear, gas coordination — all of it."
- "You need reliable power. We've been doing this in Roseburg since 2019."

### What it does NOT sound like
- "We are your premier electrical solutions provider committed to excellence."
- "Trust our team of experts to energize your future!"
- "Powering your dreams since 2019."
- Anything with "seamless journey" or "peace of mind" (overused)

### Tone Modifiers
- Clear over clever
- Specific over vague
- Confident over modest
- Local over generic
- Technical credibility over buzzwords

## Copy Rules

1. Always include "Roseburg, OR" in the first 100 words of every page.
2. Always mention CCB# 228668 on core pages (Home, About, Contact).
3. Every page ends with a CTA — never a dead end.
4. No filler sentences. If a paragraph doesn't add information, cut it.
5. Write FAQ answers as if you're talking to a homeowner — not a contractor.
6. Service page descriptions: lead with what the customer gets, then how you do it.
7. Never write "we are committed to..." or "we strive to..." — say what you do, not what you try to do.
